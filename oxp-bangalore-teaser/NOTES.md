# Notes: assets, audio cue points, decisions

## From the brand kit (used as-is)

Everything below comes from the **Odoo Experience India** design system (the artifact this video was briefed against) or the Odoo brand assets folder it was built from. None of it was redrawn or recoloured beyond the palette variants the kit already defines.

- **Odoo wordmark**, negative (all white) on night and purple grounds. Path data from the kit's `Logo` component.
- **Night ground** `night-ground.jpg` (teal glow top-left, plum glow right), scaled to cover and never rebuilt as a gradient.
- **Fonts**: Inter (Light 300, Regular 400, Bold 700) and Caveat Bold (lowercase only), from the kit's font files.
- **Colours**: Primary Purple `#714B67` (flashes, "Odoo 20", "Expert sessions" pill, the "more." ground, the metro line, the map fill), white ink, and the four additional colours (yellow, teal, cyan, red), used only for highlights, pops, doodle fills, confetti and accent lines. No gradients between palette colours, and no Secondary Green text on purple.
- **Official graphic elements** (kit `Mark` set): `underline-scribble` (under "loves" and the city in the lockup), `highlight-bar-3` (behind "you", text kept dark), `circle-bold` (around "community"), `heart`, `star`, `sparkle-1`, `pop-fan`, `arrow-curve-up`.
- **Caveat words, one per moment, each with its graphic element**: "loves" (scribble), "you" (yellow bar), "community" (circle), "bangalore" (scribble, as in the kit's `EventLockup`).
- **Event lockup**: logo + "Experience" (Inter Bold at 0.98x the logo height, gap = half the logo height, the logo's safe zone) + city in Caveat under "Experience", exactly as the kit's `EventLockup` is built.
- **Kolam**: the kit's own generator (pulli grid, one continuous line on a 5 x 6 grid), behind "more." and bleeding off the end card's top-right corner. It draws once, over 2.4 s, as the kit's motion note says.
- **Pulli** dot-grid texture, flashing on the opening kicks and on the beats under "So we are back."
- **Metro route motif** (kit `RouteAgenda`): a Primary Purple line with white-disc stations carries "The Odoo Experience is on tour." to the Bangalore stop.
- **Doodles**: `auto-rickshaw` and `filter-coffee` (Bengaluru set, night ink) on the end card, in the right third and clear of the type.
- **App icons** on white tiles with the night tile shadow: Sales, CRM, Inventory, Website, Point of Sale ("Live demos").

## Made for this video

These were created here, in the brand's line and colour language, because the kit doesn't include them:

- **India outline** (`src/brand/india.json`): built from Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 0, **India point-of-view** dataset (public domain), so the boundary matches India's official map, including all of Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and Arunachal Pradesh. Equirectangular projection at 22°N, simplified to about 1,000 points. Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep are included. The map draws itself starting from Gujarat and fills with Primary Purple.
- **Vidhana Soudha line art** (`src/brand/vidhanaSoudha.ts`): a front elevation with the main dome, lantern and lion capital, the drum, twelve columns, the grand staircase, three-storey wings, wing domes and corner chhatris. Each stroke has its own slot on a 0..1 trace clock. Scene 3 stops at 48% (dome, drum and the central columns, cropped and low opacity). Scene 6 pulls back from that exact crop and completes the drawing.
- **Hearts and pins** in Scene 1: the kit's official heart pop rising from 18 cities; 40+ city pins that converge on Gandhinagar (Odoo Experience's home in Gujarat) and pack into one cluster.
- **Confetti and radiating shapes**: palette-coloured pieces mixed with the kit's star, sparkle and heart pops, plus radial strokes and rings (the pop-fan idea at full scale) for "to you" and "more.".
- **"Networking" line**: one teal line through ten white dots, the "one line, every dot" idea.
- **Grain**: a fine animated noise over the frame so the dark gradients don't band after H.264 compression.
- **Soundtrack**: a procedural placeholder (see below).

## Decisions to confirm

- **Bangalore vs Bengaluru.** The brief's copy says "Bangalore" and must not change, so the video uses "Bangalore" throughout. The design system's lockup default is "bengaluru". Swap the word in `Scene6Outro.tsx` if the city name should follow the kit.
- **Lockup.** No standalone "Odoo Experience" logo file was in the brand folder, so the end card uses the kit's `EventLockup` construction (wordmark + "Experience" + city).
- **End-card details.** Space is left under "Coming soon." Fill `END_CARD.dateLine` / `END_CARD.detailLine` in `src/config.ts` once the date or venue is confirmed (for example "12 Dec 2026 · 10:00 AM IST").
- **Other formats.** 1:1 and 16:9 render from the same scenes with relative layouts. 4:5 is the art-directed version.
